Konkan Slant Roof Rain Defense
1
38° Sloping Pitch: Chiplun receives >3,500 mm annual monsoon precipitation. Steep pitch prevents pooling, algae formation, and water seepage.
2
Mangalore Red Clay Tiles: Interlocking terracotta tiles channel heavy downpours rapidly while providing natural passive thermal cooling in hot coastal summers.
3
3.5 ft Deep Eaves (Chajjas): Extra-wide overhangs shield exterior plaster and open wooden balconies from aggressive driving coastal monsoon winds.
4
Laterite Stone Plinth: 2.5 ft elevated foundation base built from local porous Chira (red laterite) stone safeguards against ground splashing and flooding.

Strict Architectural Compliance

Floor Plan Priority: 30'×30' footprint, 14' 1½" left front porch with 2 columns, double-height living room (241 ft²), open-to-below void, 90 ft² doglegged stairs with store, ground bedroom, kitchen (184 ft²) with side door, first-floor front balcony & side balcony exactly matching Drawing A100.
P2
Exterior Reference Style: 38° sloping Mangalore clay tile roof with hipped eaves and dormer ridge, warm beige stone/brick cladding panels, cream stucco walls, dark teakwood columns with charupadi bench seating, and dark-framed windows.
